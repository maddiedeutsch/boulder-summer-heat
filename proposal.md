**Team:** Madeleine Deutsch and Dustin

## Research question
Has the number of 90°F+ days per summer in Boulder, Colorado changed from 1980 to 2025?

## Data
Historical daily high temperatures for Boulder, collected from the web.

## Plan
- Collect daily temperature records for 1980 to 2025 (Dustin)
- Count 90°F+ days for each summer and look for a trend (both)
- Build charts comparing recent and earlier summers (Madeleine)
